# Great Courses

This collection highlights standout university courses, authoritative MOOCs, and specialized professional training series in Library and Information Studies (LIS). These audiovisual deep-dives and self-paced programs build practical skills and conceptual models across metadata systems, data curation, information policy, and community engagement.

## Knowledge Organization, Metadata, and Information Architecture

- [Catalogers Learning Workshop: LCSH, LCC, and RDA Training Modules (Library of Congress / Program for Cooperative Cataloging)](https://www.loc.gov/catworkshop/)
- [Text Retrieval and Search Engines (Coursera / University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign / Prof. ChengXiang Zhai)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/text-retrieval)
- [Information Retrieval Lecture Series (University of Freiburg / Prof. Dr. Hannah Bast)](https://ad-wiki.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/teaching/InformationRetrievalWS2223)
- [Designing Information Architecture Specialization (Coursera / Pabini Gabriel-Petit)](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/designing-information-architecture)
- [Knowledge Graphs - Foundations and Applications (openHPI / Hasso Plattner Institute / Prof. Dr. Harald Sack)](https://open.hpi.de/courses/knowledgegraphs2020)

## Digital Stewardship, Data Management, and Preservation

- [Digital Collections Stewardship (WebJunction / Washington State University Center for Digital Scholarship and Curation)](https://learn.webjunction.org/)
